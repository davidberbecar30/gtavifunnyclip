gtavifunnyclip

gtavifunnyclip is a private desktop tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @gtavifunnyclip. It finds funny moments in public GTA VI live streams, cuts them into short vertical clips, and lets the creator review each clip before it is posted.

When the creator approves a clip, the tool uses the TikTok Content Posting API to send it to the creator's own TikTok account, either as a draft to finish in the TikTok app or as a direct post at a time the creator schedules. The creator signs in once with Login Kit.

The tool is not offered to the public and only posts to the account that authorized it.
