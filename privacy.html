Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

gtavifunnyclip ("the app") is a personal desktop tool that posts the operator's own clips to the operator's own TikTok account.

What the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token, used only to upload videos to that account.
  • Upload and post status for videos the app sent.

Where it is stored

Tokens and post links are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's. The app has no user accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and does not sell or share data.

Deleting data

Revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services; the operator can also delete the stored token file at any time.
